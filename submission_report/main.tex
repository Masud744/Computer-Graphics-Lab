\textbf{Problem Description:}
\vspace{0.08cm}
{\small Draw a face enclosed in a black circle (radius 300), small green blots for eyes, a thick blue triangle for a nose, and a red smiling mouth. The smiling mouth is synthesized by drawing a red blot, and then overlaying a slightly higher white blot to leave a red crescent.}

\vspace{0.35cm}
\textbf{Methodology:}
\vspace{0.08cm}
{\small The face boundary is drawn with \texttt{circle(300)} from $(0, -300)$. The crescent smile is formed via color occlusion: a red disk (\texttt{dot(180)}) at $(0, -90)$ is overlaid with an offset white disk at $(0, -65)$. The mouth is drawn before the triangular nose and green eyes so the white blot does not overwrite foreground features.}

\vspace{0.35cm}
\textbf{Output Visualization:}
\vspace{0.1cm}
\begin{center}
\includegraphics[width=0.88\textwidth]{screenshots/task1_output.png}
\end{center}

\vspace{0.15cm}
\textbf{Result \& Observation:}
\vspace{0.08cm}
{\small The mouth must be rendered prior to the nose and eyes. Proper Z-ordering ensures the crescent smile is cleanly carved out without overlapping the foreground nasal and eye contours.}
\end{minipage}

{\small Draw the shape consisting of two overlapping squares sharing the identical center point $(0,0)$. One square is aligned horizontally and vertically ($0^\circ$), and the second square is rotated by $45^\circ$, forming an 8-pointed star shape.}

\vspace{0.35cm}
\textbf{Methodology:}
\vspace{0.08cm}
{\small The upright square of side length $S = 200$ is drawn from corner $(-100, -100)$. The $45^\circ$ rotated square is drawn with corner offset $R = \frac{S}{2}\sqrt{2} \approx 141.42$ along the vertical axis. Pen is set to $(0, -R)$ and directed via \texttt{setheading(45)} before drawing 4 equal sides.}
